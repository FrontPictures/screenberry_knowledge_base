# Timeline View and Navigation

![Timelineview](/images/media-playback/static/timelineview.png)

The timeline panel offers a range of tools and options for organizing your media and managing playback. These include the following:

1. **Current Time Display**: Displays the current playhead’s time position on the timeline.
2. **Guide Position Display**: Displays the time position of the guide on the timeline.
3. **Top Toolbar**: Contains tools for adding timeline tracks, inserting various types of markers, and managing the zoom level of the timeline view.
4. **Side Toolbar**: Contains the selectors for the **Razor** <img alt="Razor Icon" src="/images/media-playback/static/razor-icon.png" width={23} height={23} loading="lazy" /> and **Move** <img alt="Move Icon" src="/images/media-playback/static/move-icon.png" width={23} height={23} loading="lazy" /> tools, the **Playhead Snapping** toggle <img alt="Playheadsnap Icon" src="/images/media-playback/static/playheadsnap-icon.png" width={23} height={23} loading="lazy" />, the **Snap On/Off** switch <img alt="Snap Icon" src="/images/media-playback/static/snap-icon.png" width={23} height={23} loading="lazy" /> for controlling the snapping of media items, and the timeline **Preferences** .
5. **Tracks**: Serve to organize and layer media items on the timeline.
6. **Time Ruler**: Provides a time scale for precise media alignment.
7. **Playhead**: Indicates the present playback position on the timeline.
8. **Guide**: Serves as a reference for a specific time position on the timeline.
9. **Media Items**: Individual media items positioned within the timeline tracks.
10. **Playback Controls Bar**: Contains the **Play Selected Item** <img alt="Play" src="/images/media-playback/static/play.png" width={24} height={24} loading="lazy" />, **Play** <img alt="Play1" src="/images/media-playback/static/play1.png" width={24} height={24} loading="lazy" />, **Pause** <img alt="Pause" src="/images/media-playback/static/pause.png" width={24} height={24} loading="lazy" />, and **Stop** <img alt="Stop" src="/images/media-playback/static/stop.png" width={24} height={24} loading="lazy" /> buttons for managing playback. Additionally, it includes the **Lock/Unlock Playback Controls** toggle <img alt="Lock" src="/images/media-playback/static/lock.png" width={24} height={24} loading="lazy" /> and the **Reveal Active Item** button <img alt="Reveal Icon" src="/images/media-playback/static/reveal-icon.png" width={23} height={23} loading="lazy" />.
11. **Media Player’s Volume Control**: Adjusts the output volume of the media player (see [Volume Control](../media-player/media-player-global-settings#volume-control)).
12. **Media Player’s Color Correction**: Adjusts the color parameters of the media player’s video output (see [Color Correction](../media-player/media-player-global-settings#color-correction)).

## Positioning the playhead on a timeline

To position the playhead on a timeline, do any of the following:

- Click on the time ruler where you wish to position the playhead.
- Drag the playhead in the time ruler. If you want the playhead to snap to media item edges and markers, enable the **Playhead Snapping** toggle <img alt="Playheadsnap Icon" src="/images/media-playback/static/playheadsnap-icon.png" width={23} height={23} loading="lazy" /> in the side toolbar.
- Use the arrow keys. Press **`Right Arrow`** to move to the next frame and **`Left Arrow`** to move to the previous frame. Press **`Down Arrow`** to move to the next media item edge or marker and **`Up Arrow`** to move to the previous edge or marker.
- Press **`K`** to move the playhead to the next keyframe and **`J`** to move it to the previous keyframe.
- Press **`Home`** to move the playhead to the beginning of the timeline and **`End`** to move it to the end.

## Zooming in or out of a timeline

To zoom in or out of a timeline, do one of the following:

- With the mouse cursor over a timeline, use the mouse’s scroll wheel to zoom in or out based on the mouse cursor’s position.
- Press the plus **`+`** key to zoom in and the minus **`–`** key to zoom out based on the playhead position.
- Click the <img alt="Zoomplus Icon" src="/images/media-playback/static/zoomplus-icon.png" width={23} height={23} loading="lazy" /> or <img alt="Zoomminus Icon" src="/images/media-playback/static/zoomminus-icon.png" width={23} height={23} loading="lazy" /> buttons in the top toolbar to zoom in or out based on the playhead position.
- Click the <img alt="Zoomfit Icon" src="/images/media-playback/static/zoomfit-icon.png" width={23} height={23} loading="lazy" /> button in the top toolbar or press the **`\`** (backslash) key to zoom out and reveal the entire timeline.
- Click the <img alt="Zoomframe Icon" src="/images/media-playback/static/zoomframe-icon.png" width={23} height={23} loading="lazy" /> button in the top toolbar or press the **`Shift`** + **`\`** (backslash) keys to zoom in to the frame level.

<img alt="Zooming In Or Out Of A Timeline 1" src="/images/media-playback/animation/Zooming-in-or-out-of-a-timeline-1.gif" width={800} height={400} loading="lazy" />

## Scrolling a timeline horizontally

When parts of a timeline are out of view, you can scroll it horizontally by dragging the scroll bar at the bottom of the timeline window.
