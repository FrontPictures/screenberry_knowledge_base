# Overview

The **3D Scene** widget in Screenberry allows you to create and manipulate three-dimensional environments. It's used for various purposes, including:

- **3D Mapping**: Applying UV mapping and projection mapping to 3D objects and models.
- **Content Remapping**: Easily remapping images or textures onto basic 3D shapes, such as spheres or cylinders.
- **Environment Simulations**: Simulating real-world projection setups, for example, in projection studies.

Within the **3D Scene** widget, you can manipulate objects, manage cameras and projection mappers (projectors), and apply and blend textures.
