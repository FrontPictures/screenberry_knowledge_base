# Timeline Playback

To start timeline playback, do one of the following:

- Click the **Play** button <img alt="Play1" src="/images/media-playback/static/play1.png" width={24} height={24} loading="lazy" /> to start playback from the current playhead position.
- Select a media item on the timeline track and click the **Play Selected Item** button <img alt="Play" src="/images/media-playback/static/play.png" width={24} height={24} loading="lazy" /> (or press **`Ctrl`** + **`Spacebar`**) to start timeline playback beginning with that item.
- Hold down the **`Alt`** key while hovering over the timeline tracks, which will change the mouse cursor into an orange **Play** symbol ![Altplay](/images/media-playback/static/altplay.png), and then click on the media item or the marker you want to start playback from.
- If the timeline was previously paused, you can press the **`Spacebar`** to resume playback from the current playhead position.

To pause the timeline playback, you have the following options:

- Click the **Pause** button <img alt="Pause" src="/images/media-playback/static/pause.png" width={24} height={24} loading="lazy" /> to pause the playback at the current playhead position.
- Press the **`Spacebar`** to quickly pause or resume playback. This keyboard shortcut works as a toggle — each press will switch between playing and pausing the timeline.

To stop the timeline playback, click the **Stop** button <img alt="Stop" src="/images/media-playback/static/stop.png" width={24} height={24} loading="lazy" /> to halt playback completely. This action will also reset the playhead position to the beginning of the timeline.

Additionally, the **Playback Controls** bar consists of **Lock/Unlock Playback Controls** toggle <img alt="Lock" src="/images/media-playback/static/lock.png" width={24} height={24} loading="lazy" /> and the **Reveal Active Item** button <img alt="Reveal Icon" src="/images/media-playback/static/reveal-icon.png" width={23} height={23} loading="lazy" />.

To prevent accidental changes from being made to a timeline’s playback state, you can enable the **Lock/Unlock** **Controls** toggle <img alt="Lock" src="/images/media-playback/static/lock.png" width={24} height={24} loading="lazy" />. When this control is locked, all play, pause, and stop buttons will become disabled. To unlock the playback controls, click the **Lock/Unlock Playback Controls** toggle <img alt="Lock" src="/images/media-playback/static/lock.png" width={24} height={24} loading="lazy" /> again.

To locate the currently playing media item, click the **Reveal Active Item** button <img alt="Reveal Icon" src="/images/media-playback/static/reveal-icon.png" width={23} height={23} loading="lazy" />. Upon clicking, the view will be switched to the active timeline, which will be automatically scrolled to the position of the currently playing item, and the item will be highlighted for easy identification.
