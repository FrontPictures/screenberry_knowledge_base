# Privacy Policy

### Privacy and Data Security

Front Pictures LLC understands that the right to privacy and security is a major concern. We use the guidelines below in order to protect your personal information. Screenberry Privacy Policy We collect, store and use your personal information for defined purposes. This information is used to support and enhance our relationship with you, for example, to provide tailored service and support, share product, service and company news and offerings with you. We do not sell your personal information, but we may share your personal information with our distributors and partners to ensure you get the best possible experience from Screenberry. Please contact Screenberry team if you have privacy questions or concerns. You also may ask to see the personal data you have given us and request amendment, correction or deletion.

### What information do we collect?

We ask for specific types of personal information. We ask you to provide information that will enable us to process your order or provide services to you, enhance your site visit, respond to requests for information, assist you with technical support issues or to follow up with you after your visit.

We collect information when you interact with us. Some of the instances when this happens might be when you:

Visit a Screenberry website

Subscribe to a publication such as a newsletter or a mailing list

Request us to provide you with technical assistance

Place an order with us

Request e-mail notification from us

Make a request for support

Provide feedback in a survey

When you interact with us, we may ask for some, or all, of the following information:

your IP address

your name

email address

phone number

occupation

company

transactional information (such as product part number, serial number, service history or any other information needed to process your transaction or request)

The purpose is to offer you a more consistent and personalized experience in your relationship with Screenberry.

### Use of personal information

The information you provide will be kept confidential (see "Screenberry's Privacy Policy" above). It is collected and processed for the following general purposes or for other lawful business purposes:

To process your order or purchase

To fulfil requests for products and services

To improve our products and services

To contact you

To customize our communication with you and the marketing material we share with you

To allow our business partners to contact you

### What about cookies?

A cookie is a small file containing a string of characters that is transmitted to your computer when you visit a website. If you then visit the website again, the cookie allows this page, for example, to recognise your browser again. Cookies are not usually used to store personal data, but can store user preferences and other information. You can set your browser to reject all cookies or to inform you when a cookie is sent. Please follow the instructions in the help function of your browser regarding the prevention and deletion of cookies. We use cookies to enable our systems to recognise the terminal device or browser you are using and to provide you with our services. Some functionalities or services of the website may not function properly without cookies and we therefore recommend that you accept cookies so that you can make full use of our website. For more information about cookies and how we use them, please read our cookies notice.
